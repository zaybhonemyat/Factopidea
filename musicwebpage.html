Oasis

Oasis surprisingly Split & Reunion

Oasis officially split in 2009 after a major disagreement. The band later reunited and became active again by 2024.

Record-Breaking Album

Their third album, Be Here Now, became the fastest-selling album in British history.

The Origin Behind the Name “Oasis”

The band was originally called The Rain. Liam renamed it after seeing “Oasis” on a poster in his room.

Noel Gallagher

Noel wrote many of Oasis’ biggest hits, including Wonderwall and Don’t Look Back in Anger, totaling over 190 songs.

‘Supersonic’ Made in a Single Day

The band wrote and recorded Supersonic in just one day while in a Liverpool studio.

The Gallagher Brothers

Liam and Noel Gallagher are siblings and served as the core members of Oasis.

The Dispute That Stopped the Tour

A backstage conflict during a tour led to Noel stepping away and the rest of the shows being cancelled.

Keshi

Keshi: His ethnicity

Keshi is known for lo-fi R&B with emotional, soothing melodies.

From Nurse to Musician

He switched from oncology nursing to a full-time music career after a difficult experience at his hospital job.

How “Keshi” Got His Name

The name comes from a Japanese pronunciation of “Casey,” given by a childhood friend’s parents.

Keshi on Fame & Fan Messages

He shared that he gets many fan messages daily. It can be tiring, but he appreciates replying.

Keshi’s Signature music style

His music blends Neo-Soul and R&B with complex jazz-influenced guitar chords.

Childhood love

Keshi and Mai have been together since childhood. They got engaged in 2020 and now live together in Texas.

Self-Taught

He learned guitar using his grandfather’s classical guitar and a Vietnamese music book.

Early Talent & Hobbies

He learned piano as a kid, played in orchestra, and also enjoys gaming like Valorant.

Tattoos and its meaning

He has a full sleeve with tigers and waves, and another tattoo representing his family’s zodiac signs.
